OUR BEGINNING

A magazine built by students.

Red Shoes Review was established in 1970 and has been part of UIC's creative community for more than five decades.

Throughout its history, the magazine has evolved while continuing to provide students with a space to create, collaborate, and share their work.

The magazine has changed over the years, but its commitment to student creativity remains.

1970

Red Shoes Review Begins

Red Shoes Review was established in 1970, beginning a student-led tradition of celebrating creative expression at the University of Illinois Chicago.

OUR EARLY YEARS

The Honors College

RSR was originally associated with UIC's Honors College, where the magazine developed as a space for students to share their writing, art, and creative perspectives.

A NEW CHAPTER

Becoming Independent

Red Shoes Review later became an independent student organization, opening a new chapter in the magazine's history and expanding the community surrounding it.

RSR TODAY

Creativity Without Boundaries

Today, Red Shoes Review is built around the idea that creativity is not limited to one college, major, or discipline. Students across UIC can take part in the magazine and contribute to the creative community that continues to shape RSR.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

“Creativity belongs to everyone.”
