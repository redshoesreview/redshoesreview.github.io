01 / ABOUT

WHO WE ARE

A magazine made for student voices.

Red Shoes Review is a student-run literary and arts magazine at the University of Illinois Chicago. We publish and celebrate creative work from students across disciplines.

Think poetry tucked into margins, photographs that feel like memories, essays that refuse to behave, and art that deserves to be held onto.
